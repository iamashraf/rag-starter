# Engineering bands

Staff Engineer (L6) base salary band: 210,000 to 265,000 USD.
Principal Engineer (L7) base salary band: 250,000 to 320,000 USD.

# Review cycle

Bands are reviewed every January against market data. Managers must not share band
details with candidates or employees outside the formal offer process.
