# Scope

This policy applies to all full-time employees and contractors traveling on company business.

# Meal limits

| Location tier | Daily meal limit (USD) |
|---------------|------------------------|
| Tier 1 (NYC, SF, London) | 95 |
| Tier 2 (Austin, Denver, Toronto) | 75 |
| Tier 3 (all other) | 60 |

Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of a pre-approved client dinner.

# Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in the Expensify app within 30 days of the trip end date. Attach itemized
receipts for any single expense over 25 USD. Expenses submitted after 60 days are not
reimbursed without VP approval.

# Flights

Book economy for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or
more. Business class requires approval from your VP and Finance.
